1. The service

Belline answers telephone calls you forward to it, the voice button and chat on your website, and a second WhatsApp number you register with Belline, which we set up with you. Your own WhatsApp stays as it is. Belline answers questions from the information you give it, takes messages and booking requests, puts urgent calls through to a team number where you have set one, and writes a summary and transcript of every conversation. Where you have connected a supported booking system or calendar, it checks availability and makes bookings in it. No booking system or calendar can be connected yet, so today your team confirms every booking request. Belline answers in English. Features differ by plan, as shown on the pricing page.

2. The free trial

30 days free: 30 voice minutes and 50 text conversations, with the phone, the voice button on your website, your website chat and WhatsApp all switched on. No card, nothing charged. Standard onboarding is free.

A trial's voice minutes and text conversations are a limit, whether or not card payments are open. When its voice minutes are used, Belline stops answering calls and the voice button on your website. When its text conversations are used, it stops replying on your website chat and WhatsApp. Once card payments are open, Belline also stops answering when a trial ends, until you choose a plan. Card payments are not open yet. Until they are, a trial is not stopped by its end date alone: Belline keeps answering within the trial's voice minutes and text conversations, and we contact you about your first payment.

When an allowance runs out, you choose what happens: add a pack automatically (100 extra voice minutes for AED 99, or 150 extra text conversations for AED 49), up to a monthly spending cap you set; move up to the next plan; or stop at the allowance. We tell you at 70%, 90% and 100%. Nothing is added to your bill unless you chose it.

  • We may change prices with at least 30 days' notice by email. A change applies from your next billing period.
