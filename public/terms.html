1. The service

Belline answers your telephone calls that are forwarded to it, and conversations on your website and connected messaging channels. It answers questions from the information you give it, checks availability in the Belline diary, makes, changes and cancels bookings, takes messages, puts urgent calls through to a number you choose, and sends booking confirmations and reminders by text. Features differ by plan, as shown on the pricing page.

2. The free trial

A trial lasts 14 days and includes 30 minutes of live calls. No card is needed and nothing is charged. When the trial ends or its minutes are used, Belline stops answering until you choose a plan.
