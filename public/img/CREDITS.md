# Photography

Placeholders, not the final set.

Every picture here is from Pexels and free to use commercially without
attribution. They are in the repository because the three images they replaced
were visibly AI-generated — invented dentists, invented stylists — on a site
whose whole argument is that Belline does not pretend. Invented people were the
wrong furniture for that argument, whatever they looked like.

What they should eventually be, and why, is in the photo brief. The short
version: the picture that sells this is the phone ringing while every pair of
hands is busy — a detail, close, in available light, nobody looking at the
camera. Free libraries do not have that moment; Stocksy does, and a half-day
with a photographer in three real Dubai venues does it better and exclusively.
