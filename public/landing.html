What counts as a voice minute

A voice minute is time Belline spends on a live call with your caller — from the moment it answers to the moment the call ends — rounded up to the next whole minute. Calls you make from your own test console do not count, and neither do calls cut short by a fault on our side.

No surprise invoices

There is no per-minute charge on any plan. The invoice is the plan fee and nothing else — if the allowance runs short, the answer is a bigger plan, not a bigger bill.

Questions

What operators ask first.

Do we have to change our phone number?

No. You keep the number on your signage and your listings, and set call forwarding on the line you already have. You can turn it off yourself at any time.

What happens if we run out of minutes?

Belline keeps answering. A receptionist that stops picking up because of an invoice is not a receptionist, so we do not build one — your dashboard tells you that you have gone past, and which plan would cover it. You are never charged a penny more than the plan fee for that.

Will it answer calls our staff would have taken?

Only the ones nobody reaches. It sits behind your line: it picks up when the phone has rung unanswered, or when the line is engaged. Your team always gets first refusal.

Are we tied in?

No. Monthly plans are cancelled whenever you like and run to the end of the period you have paid for. Annual is two months free in exchange for paying up front. Standard onboarding costs nothing.

What do you do with our guests’ data?

Transcripts and bookings are stored so you can audit what was said; call audio is not recorded. The data belongs to your venue and is exportable. We are happy to sign whatever your legal counsel requires.
