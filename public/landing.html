What counts as a minute

A minute is time Belline spends on a live call with your caller — on your phone line or through the voice button on your website — from the moment it answers to the moment the call ends, rounded up to the next whole minute. Calls you make from your own test console do not count, and neither do calls cut short by a fault on our side.

What counts as a conversation

A conversation is one customer's thread in your website chat or on WhatsApp in which Belline replies at least once. Everything that customer says in the 24 hours after Belline's first reply is the same conversation. Threads your team answers without Belline do not count.

When an allowance runs out

When an allowance runs out, you choose what happens: add a pack automatically (100 extra voice minutes for AED 99, or 150 extra text conversations for AED 49), up to a monthly spending cap you set; move up to the next plan; or stop at the allowance. We tell you at 70%, 90% and 100%. Nothing is added to your bill unless you chose it.

What owners ask first.

Do we have to change our phone number?

No. You keep your number and set call forwarding on the line you already have. You can turn it off yourself at any time.

Does it work with Fresha, SevenRooms or OpenTable?

Not yet. Those connections need a partner agreement with each company, and we don’t have one yet. Today, Belline answers your customers, takes their details and request, and your team books it in your system as usual. Tell us which system you use.

Will it sound like a robot?

Speak to it and decide. The button on this page reaches the same receptionist your customers would. If someone asks whether it’s an AI, it says so.

What if it gets something wrong?

It answers only from your information. If it doesn’t know, it takes a message. Every conversation has a summary and a full transcript, so you can see exactly what was said.

Can it put a caller through to us?

Yes. Give Belline a number for your team and it puts urgent calls through live. If nobody picks up, the caller is told the team will ring back, and the call is at the top of your list.

Will it answer calls our staff would have taken?

Only if you forward them. Most businesses forward calls when nobody answers or the line is busy, so the team still picks up first.

Does it send reminders or confirmations?

Not yet. Belline doesn’t send texts or emails to your customers today. When it does, you’ll be able to switch them on.

What happens if we use up our allowance?

When an allowance runs out, you choose what happens: add a pack automatically (100 extra voice minutes for AED 99, or 150 extra text conversations for AED 49), up to a monthly spending cap you set; move up to the next plan; or stop at the allowance. We tell you at 70%, 90% and 100%. Nothing is added to your bill unless you chose it.

Does call forwarding cost us anything?

It can, and your phone provider decides how much. Belline answers on a number of its own. If that number is outside the UAE, your provider may charge an international rate for each forwarded call. The dashboard shows the exact number.

Are we tied in?

No. Monthly plans cancel any time and run to the end of the paid period. Annual plans are paid up front and include 2 months free.

We’re not a clinic, salon or restaurant. Can we still use it?

Yes. If customers ring or message you with questions, Belline can answer from your information. Pick the closest trade when you sign up.

What do you do with our customers’ data?

Transcripts and messages are stored so you can check what was said. Call audio is not recorded. The data belongs to your business, and we hand it over if you leave.

Does it answer WhatsApp?

Yes, on a second number. You register a new WhatsApp number with Belline from your dashboard, and Belline answers it from your information and passes requests to your team. Your own WhatsApp stays as it is.

Does it speak Arabic?

Not yet. Belline answers in English.
