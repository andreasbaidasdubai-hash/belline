What counts as a voice minute

A voice minute is time Belline spends on a live call with your caller — from the moment it answers to the moment the call ends — rounded up to the next whole minute. Calls you make from your own test console do not count, and neither do calls cut short by a fault on our side.

Questions

What operators ask first.

Do we have to change our phone number?

No. You keep the number on your signage and your listings, and set call forwarding on the line you already have. You can turn it off yourself at any time.

Does it work with Fresha, SevenRooms or OpenTable?

Not yet, and we won’t pretend otherwise. Belline keeps its own diary — you and your team work in it, and it is the diary the receptionist books into. A one-way feed into Google Calendar is built and waiting on Google’s approval. The booking-platform integrations need a partner agreement with each of them; we have not got those yet. If your whole business lives in one of them, the honest advice today is to try Belline on the trial and see whether the diary is enough.

Will it sound like a robot?

Ring it and decide — the number and the bell on this page reach the same receptionist your callers would. It speaks like a person, it says “sure” and “let me see”, and it will not read you a script. On the demo line it tells you it is an AI; on your line, what it says about itself is your choice.

What if it books something wrong?

It can only offer a time that is actually free — availability comes from the booking engine, not from the conversation, so it cannot invent a slot. Every booking has the full transcript beside it. If it is unsure of a name, a date or a number it asks again rather than guessing, and anything outside its rules becomes a message for you instead of a booking.

Can it put a caller through to us?

Not live, not yet. When a call needs a person it takes the number and the reason, marks it urgent if it is, and ends the call so you can ring back with the whole story in front of you. Live transfer is on the way; we will say so here when it works.

We’re not a clinic, salon or restaurant — can we still use it?

Yes. If people ring you to book a time — a physio, a vet, a garage, a studio, a tutor, a barber — Belline answers, takes the booking against your diary and knows your hours and prices. The four trades on this page are the ones whose particular rules are built in already: patch tests, kitchen pacing, surgeries as a separate constraint. Everyone else picks the closest trade at sign-up and tells Belline their own rules in plain words. If yours is unusual, email us and we’ll set it up with you.

Does it speak Arabic?

Not yet. It answers in English today. Arabic is planned, but nobody has made a real Arabic call on it, so we are not selling you one.

What happens if we run out of minutes?

Belline keeps answering. A receptionist that stops picking up because of an invoice is not a receptionist, so we do not build one — your dashboard tells you that you have gone past, and which plan would cover it. You are never charged a dirham more than the plan fee for that.

Will it answer calls our staff would have taken?

Only the ones nobody reaches. It sits behind your line: it picks up when the phone has rung unanswered, or when the line is engaged. Your team always gets first refusal.

Are we tied in?

No. Monthly plans are cancelled whenever you like and run to the end of the period you have paid for. Annual is two months free in exchange for paying up front. Standard onboarding costs nothing.

What do you do with our guests’ data?

Transcripts and bookings are stored so you can audit what was said; call audio is not recorded. The data belongs to your venue, and we hand all of it over if you leave. We are happy to sign whatever your legal counsel requires.
