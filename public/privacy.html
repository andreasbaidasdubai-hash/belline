Privacy policy

Last updated 17 September 2026

Belline answers the telephone, website chat and WhatsApp messages for businesses. Doing that means handling the details of the people who contact those businesses. This page says what we collect, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

Belline is operated by Belline, reachable at hello@belline.ai. For anything on this page, write to hello@belline.ai.

Two kinds of people, two roles

Businesses that use Belline — the owners and staff who sign in. For their account details we decide what is collected and why, so we are responsible for that data.

Their customers — people who ring, chat or message a business that uses Belline. Here the business decides why the data is used; we process it on its behalf and on its instructions. If you are one of those customers, the business you contacted is your first point of contact, and we will help it answer you.

What we collect

When somebody contacts a business through Belline

  • The phone number they call from, the WhatsApp number they message from, or the chat they write in.
  • What is said or written, kept as a text transcript, together with a short summary.
  • The details of any request they make: their name and number, what they asked for, when they would like it, notes, and an email address where the business needs one.
  • Messages taken for the business, and whether the call was put through to a person.

Call audio is not recorded, with one exception the caller chooses. The voice is turned into text as the call happens, and the audio itself is not kept. A voice note sent in website chat is turned into text the same way, and the recording is not kept.

Video calls on a business's website. No business can offer video calls yet. When one does, the visitor talks to an AI concierge shown as an AI-generated face, never a real person's. The visitor's voice is sent to Tavus, which turns it into text and gives the face its voice, over a live connection run by Daily. The visitor's camera is never used and the call is not recorded. What is said is kept as a text transcript like any other call, and Tavus also holds a transcript of the conversation, which we can ask it to delete.

Voicemail before a business goes live. A business can forward its calls to Belline before it has switched Belline on. Until it does, Belline does not answer; a caller hears a short message and can choose to leave a voicemail with their name and number. That voicemail is a recording. It is stored by Twilio, played only to the people at that business, not turned into text, and kept on the same terms as transcripts below. A caller who does not want to be recorded can hang up before the tone.

When a business signs up

  • The business name, the type of business, the owner's name and email address, and a password, stored only as a salted hash.
  • The information the business gives Belline to answer with: opening hours, services, prices, staff, rules and common questions.
  • Price lists or brochures uploaded at setup. They are read once to draft the business's information and are not stored. Only the information the owner saves is kept.
  • Once card payments are open, billing details are handled by Stripe. We keep the plan, the billing period and Stripe's reference for the customer, never card numbers.

When you visit belline.ai

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. If you talk to or write to Belline on this site, that conversation is handled like any other call or chat above. The dashboard at app.belline.ai uses one essential cookie to keep you signed in.

The waitlist on the German-language pages. If you join the waitlist on belline.ai/de-de, /de-at or /de-ch, we store your name, email address, business name, country and, if you give one, type of business, so that we can contact you when Belline opens in your country. We send nothing when you join. The entry is kept until you ask us to delete it, which you can do at any time by writing to hello@belline.ai.

What we use it for

  • To answer the call or message and do what was asked: answer a question, take a message or a booking request, put the caller through.
  • Where the business has turned it on and it is available: messages about the customer's request or booking. None are available yet.
  • To show the business what happened, so it can check every conversation and follow up every request.
  • To recognise a returning customer by number, so they are greeted properly.
  • To run, secure, fix and bill for the service.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who helps us

Belline is built on services from other companies, each of which processes data only to provide its part:

  • Twilio — telephone lines, voicemail recordings left before a business goes live, and WhatsApp messages where a number is connected through Twilio.
  • Deepgram — turning speech into text during a call, and voice notes sent in website chat.
  • ElevenLabs — turning Belline's replies into speech.
  • Anthropic — the language model that understands the request and decides what to say, and that reads the website, price lists and brochures a business gives it at setup.
  • Tavus — only for video calls on a website, once businesses can offer them: turning the visitor's speech into text, and the AI-generated face and voice that speak Belline's replies.
  • Daily — the live audio and video connection for those video calls, on behalf of Tavus.
  • Railway — hosting for the application, its data and this website.
  • Stripe — subscription billing, once card payments are open.
  • Meta — WhatsApp messages, through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API), where a business has registered a second WhatsApp number with Belline.
  • Google — only if a business connects a Google Calendar, once that connection is available. No calendar can be connected yet.
  • Microsoft — only if a business connects an Outlook calendar, once that connection is available. Outlook calendars cannot be connected to Belline yet.

Some of these companies process data outside the United Arab Emirates, including in the United States and the European Union. We rely on their contractual commitments to protect it.

Google Calendar data

Connecting a Google Calendar is not available yet. When it is, and only if a business chooses to connect one, this is how Belline treats the information it receives from Google:

  • Belline asks for two permissions: to see and edit events on the business's calendars, and to see the list of its calendars. It uses them only to find the times already taken on the calendars the business picks, and to add and cancel the bookings Belline takes for that business.
  • Belline does not sell this information, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models.
  • Nobody at Belline reads it, unless the business asks us to help with a problem, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.
  • The connection is stored encrypted. The business can disconnect it in Belline at any time, which also withdraws Belline's access at Google.

Belline's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Outlook calendar data

Outlook calendars cannot be connected to Belline yet. When they can, and only if a business chooses to connect one, this is how Belline treats the information it receives from Microsoft:

  • Belline asks Microsoft for three permissions: to see and edit events on the business's calendars, to sign in with the account and read its basic profile, and to keep that access until the business disconnects. It uses them only to find the times already taken on the calendars the business picks, and to add, move and cancel the bookings Belline takes for that business. It reads the calendars through Microsoft Graph.
  • Belline does not sell this information, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models.
  • Nobody at Belline reads it, unless the business asks us to help with a problem, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.
  • The connection is stored encrypted. The business can disconnect it in Belline at any time, which deletes Belline's access. To remove Belline from the Microsoft account as well, the account holder (or, for a work account, the organisation's IT admin) removes it from the account's app permissions.

How long we keep it

Transcripts, requests, messages and voicemail recordings are kept for as long as the business's account is open, so the business can look back at what happened. When an account is closed, we delete its data within 90 days, except records we must keep by law, such as invoices. A business can ask us to delete specific records sooner.

How we protect it

Data travels encrypted. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Each person signed in to a business account sees only the locations they are given, and what they can change depends on their role. Credentials for connected services are stored encrypted.

What you can ask for

You can ask to see the personal data held about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted, and you can object to how it is used. If you contacted a business that uses Belline, ask that business, or write to us and we will pass it on and help. Account holders can write to us directly. We answer within 30 days.

A business that leaves Belline can have all of its data handed over.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so here and tell account holders by email before it takes effect.
