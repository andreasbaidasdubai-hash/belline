Privacy policy

Last updated 13 September 2026

Belline answers the telephone, website chat and messages for businesses that take bookings. Doing that means handling the details of the people who contact those businesses. This page says what we collect, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

Belline is operated by Belline, reachable at hello@belline.ai. For anything on this page, write to hello@belline.ai.

Two kinds of people, two roles

Businesses that use Belline — the owners and staff who sign in. For their account details we decide what is collected and why, so we are responsible for that data.

Their customers — people who ring, chat or message a business that uses Belline. Here the business decides why the data is used; we process it on its behalf and on its instructions. If you are one of those customers, the business you contacted is your first point of contact, and we will help it answer you.

What we collect

When somebody contacts a business through Belline

  • The phone number they call from, or the chat they write in.
  • What is said or written, kept as a text transcript, together with a short summary.
  • The details of any booking made, changed or cancelled: name, number, date, time, what was booked, notes, and an email address where the business needs one.
  • Messages taken for the business, and whether the call was put through to a person.
  • Where a deposit is due, whether it has been paid. Card details are entered on Stripe's page and never reach us.

Call audio is not recorded. The voice is turned into text as the call happens, and the audio itself is not kept.

When a business signs up

  • The business name, the type of business, the owner's name and email address, and a password, stored only as a salted hash.
  • The information the business gives Belline to answer with: opening hours, services, prices, staff, rules and common questions.
  • Billing details handled by Stripe. We keep the plan, the billing period and Stripe's reference for the customer, not card numbers.

When you visit belline.ai

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. If you talk to or write to Belline on this site, that conversation is handled like any other call or chat above. The dashboard at app.belline.ai uses one essential cookie to keep you signed in.

What we use it for

  • To answer the call or message and do what was asked: answer a question, make or change a booking, take a message, put the caller through.
  • To send what the booking needs: a confirmation, a reminder before the visit, and a link to pay a deposit where the business asks for one.
  • To show the business what happened, so it can check every call and booking.
  • To recognise a returning customer by number, so they are greeted properly.
  • To run, secure, fix and bill for the service.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who helps us

Belline is built on services from other companies, each of which processes data only to provide its part:

  • Twilio — telephone lines, text messages and WhatsApp.
  • Deepgram — turning speech into text during a call.
  • ElevenLabs — turning Belline's replies into speech.
  • Anthropic — the language model that understands the request and decides what to say.
  • Railway — hosting for the application and its data.
  • Vercel — hosting for this website.
  • Stripe — subscription billing and, where a business uses them, deposits.
  • Meta — WhatsApp, where a business has a WhatsApp number connected.
  • Google — only if a business connects its Google Calendar, to copy bookings into it.

Some of these companies process data outside the United Arab Emirates, including in the United States and the European Union. We rely on their contractual commitments to protect it.

How long we keep it

Transcripts, bookings and messages are kept for as long as the business's account is open, so the business can look back at what happened. When an account is closed, we delete its data within 90 days, except records we must keep by law, such as invoices. A business can ask us to delete specific records sooner.

How we protect it

Data travels encrypted. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Each person signed in to a business account sees only the venues they are given, and what they can change depends on their role. Credentials for connected services are stored encrypted.

What you can ask for

You can ask to see the personal data held about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted, and you can object to how it is used. If you contacted a business that uses Belline, ask that business, or write to us and we will pass it on and help. Account holders can write to us directly. We answer within 30 days.

A business that leaves Belline can have all of its data handed over.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so here and tell account holders by email before it takes effect.
