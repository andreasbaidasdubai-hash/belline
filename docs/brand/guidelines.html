Brand guidelines · Direction C

The bell button.

The front-desk bell becomes a button you press: one solid brass badge that is the logo, the app icon, the avatar and the shape of every primary action. Brass carries the surface, oversized Bricolage Grotesque carries the voice, and Belline looks sure of itself without raising it.

The calm, competent front-of-house professional who is always there and never oversteps. For owners of clinics, salons and restaurants in the UAE who want every enquiry answered in the tools they already use.

Logo

Ink bell on Bell Brass, brass bell on ink. The bell never leaves its badge in a lockup, and the badge never takes a colour outside these pairs. Files live in public/brand/.

Do not

  • Put white on brass (3.36:1). Words on brass are always ink.
  • Add gradients, shadows, glows, outlines, sound waves or motion lines.
  • Take the bell out of its badge in a lockup, or rotate, stretch or re-space it.
  • Set "Belline" in another face or weight, or track it looser than −0.045em.
  • Place the primary lockup straight onto a busy photograph.

Avatars and icons

Full-bleed brass square with the bell inside the central 50%, so any circular crop is safe. WhatsApp is coming soon: every WhatsApp avatar preview or caption must say so.

Iconography and photography

Icons are quiet line drawings; the bell is the only filled pictogram and only ever appears as the mark. Photography shows real front-of-house moments, not technology.

Icons

24-unit grid, 2 px stroke, round caps and joins, no fills. Ink by default, graphite on secondary rows. No sound waves, robots, sparkles, orbs or circuits. Third-party logos only for integrations that are live.

Photography

Reception counters, a salon chair between clients, a host stand before service, a clinic waiting room in daylight. Warm natural light; brass, wood, stone and linen. People busy and at ease, with written consent, credited in public/img/CREDITS.md.

Leave calm space for a headline. No stock AI imagery, glowing screens or dark neon. On photographs use the mono ivory lockup on a dark, even area.

UI foundations

Pills 999 px, panels 14 px, inputs 10 px, hairlines at ink 12%, a 4 px spacing base, no shadows on panels, and a 2 px Lagoon focus ring. The primary button carries the bell button at its leading edge.

Voice and tone

Sentence case, plain verbs, full sentences. Say what works today and nothing more; honesty is the brand.

AttentiveHears the caller out and answers the question asked.
PreciseNever offers a time the calendar did not return.
WarmSounds like a good receptionist, not a system.
DiscreetHands clinical and sensitive questions to a person.
DependableSays what works today and nothing more.
