Brand guidelines, Navy + Electric Blue

The bell button.

The front-desk bell becomes a button you press: one solid blue badge with a white bell that is the logo, the app icon, the avatar and the shape of every primary action. White grounds, navy type, calm grey body text and a single electric blue give Belline the steady look of business software an owner can trust with their phone line.

The calm, competent front-of-house professional who is always there and never oversteps. For owners of any UAE business that takes calls, messages or bookings and wants their phone and website chat answered properly, with the team told what to do next.

Logo

White bell on blue; blue bell on white for blue grounds. The name is Plus Jakarta Sans 700 in navy. The bell never leaves its badge in a lockup, and the badge never takes a colour outside these pairs. Files live in public/brand/ with the same names and URLs as before; the wordmark SVGs carry their own font subset.

Do not

  • Put navy on blue (3.22:1). Words and bells on blue are always white.
  • Add gradients, shadows, glows, outlines, sound waves or motion lines to the mark.
  • Take the bell out of its badge in a lockup, or rotate, stretch or re-space it.
  • Set "Belline" in another face or weight, or track it tighter than −0.025em.
  • Place the primary lockup straight onto a busy photograph.

Avatars and icons

Full-bleed blue square with the white bell inside the central 50%, so any circular crop is safe. WhatsApp is coming soon: every WhatsApp avatar preview or caption must say so. The OG image is white, names phone and website chat only, and carries a small blue dot grid.

Iconography and photography

Icons are quiet line drawings in blue on the website and navy in the dashboard; the bell is the only filled pictogram and only ever appears as the mark. The blue dot grid behind the website hero is decoration, never behind text. Photography shows real front-of-house moments, not technology.

Icons

24-unit grid, 2 px stroke, round caps and joins, no fills. On the website they sit in a 44 px blue-tint square inside a card. No sound waves, robots, sparkles, orbs or circuits. Third-party logos only for integrations that are live.

Photography

Reception counters, a salon chair between clients, a host stand before service, a clinic waiting room in daylight. Natural light. People busy and at ease, with written consent, credited in public/img/CREDITS.md.

Leave calm space for a headline. No stock AI imagery, glowing screens or dark neon. On photographs use the mono white lockup on a dark, even area.

UI foundations

Pills 999 px, website cards 16 px with a 1 px #DDE3F5 line, dashboard panels 14 px, inputs 10 px, a 4 px spacing base, no shadows on cards, and a 2 px blue focus ring (white on the navy band). The primary button is blue with white words and may carry the inverse bell button at its leading edge.

Voice and tone

Sentence case, plain verbs, full sentences. Say what works today and nothing more; honesty is the brand.

AttentiveHears the caller out and answers the question asked.
PreciseAnswers only from the business's own information, and says when it doesn't know.
WarmSounds like a good receptionist, not a system.
DiscreetHands clinical and sensitive questions to a person.
DependableSays what works today and nothing more.

Belline is not

An "AI from the future"A developer platformCute or cartoonLoud or salesyLuxury-snobDark or cryptoA second calendar

Estimates are called estimates. Sample data is labelled and its numbers agree with each other. No statistics, testimonials, customer logos or counters on the website. No exclamation marks, and no "seamless", "magic" or "never miss a call again".
