Brand guidelines · Ink + Indigo

The bell button.

The front-desk bell becomes a button you press: one solid indigo badge with a white bell that is the logo, the app icon, the avatar and the shape of every primary action. White grounds, ink type and a single indigo accent give Belline the calm look of business software an owner can trust with their phone line.

The calm, competent front-of-house professional who is always there and never oversteps. For owners of clinics, salons and restaurants in the UAE who want their phone and website chat answered properly, with the team told what to do next.

Logo

White bell on indigo; indigo bell on white for indigo grounds. The bell never leaves its badge in a lockup, and the badge never takes a colour outside these pairs. Files live in public/brand/ with the same names and URLs as before.

Do not

  • Put ink on indigo (2.82:1). Words and bells on indigo are always white.
  • Add gradients, shadows, glows, outlines, sound waves or motion lines.
  • Take the bell out of its badge in a lockup, or rotate, stretch or re-space it.
  • Set "Belline" in another face or weight, or track it looser than −0.045em.
  • Place the primary lockup straight onto a busy photograph.

Avatars and icons

Full-bleed indigo square with the white bell inside the central 50%, so any circular crop is safe. WhatsApp is coming soon: every WhatsApp avatar preview or caption must say so. The OG image names phone and website chat only.

Iconography and photography

Icons are quiet line drawings; the bell is the only filled pictogram and only ever appears as the mark. Photography shows real front-of-house moments, not technology.

Icons

24-unit grid, 2 px stroke, round caps and joins, no fills. Ink by default, text 2 on secondary rows. No sound waves, robots, sparkles, orbs or circuits. Third-party logos only for integrations that are live.

Photography

Reception counters, a salon chair between clients, a host stand before service, a clinic waiting room in daylight. Natural light. People busy and at ease, with written consent, credited in public/img/CREDITS.md.

Leave calm space for a headline. No stock AI imagery, glowing screens or dark neon. On photographs use the mono white lockup on a dark, even area.

UI foundations

Pills 999 px, panels 14 px, inputs 10 px, hairlines at ink 12%, a 4 px spacing base, no shadows on panels, and a 2 px indigo focus ring. The primary button is indigo with white words and may carry the inverse bell button at its leading edge.

Voice and tone

Sentence case, plain verbs, full sentences. Say what works today and nothing more; honesty is the brand.

AttentiveHears the caller out and answers the question asked.
PreciseAnswers only from the business's own information, and says when it doesn't know.
WarmSounds like a good receptionist, not a system.
DiscreetHands clinical and sensitive questions to a person.
DependableSays what works today and nothing more.

Belline is not

An "AI from the future"A developer platformCute or cartoonLoud or salesyLuxury-snobDark or cryptoA second calendar

Estimates are called estimates. Sample data is labelled and its numbers agree with each other. No exclamation marks, and no "seamless", "magic" or "never miss a call again".
