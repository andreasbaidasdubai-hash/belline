# Belline name and logo: conflict notes

First look, 15 September 2026. **This is not legal advice and not a clearance search.** It records what was checked while building the Direction C brand kit, what could not be checked, and what to put in front of a trademark lawyer before filing or spending on the brand.

## Summary

- No exact **BELLINE** trademark was found for software, telecoms or AI reception services in open web searches.
- The official registers could **not** be searched directly from this environment: UAE Ministry of Economy trademark register, EUIPO eSearch plus, and WIPO Global Brand Database. The absence of a hit above therefore proves nothing. A register search in classes 9, 35, 38 and 42 (at least), in the UAE, the GCC, the EU and the UK, is the first lawyer item.
- The protectable parts of the brand are the word **BELLINE** and the combined logo (bell button + wordmark). A service bell on its own is a common pictogram and is unlikely to be registrable or defensible by itself.

## Items for the lawyer

Status key: **Seen** = found in a public source listed below; **Unverified** = reported or suspected, not confirmed against a register.

| # | Item | Why it matters | Status |

| 1 | **Belgian entity named BELLINE**, Deinze, Oost-Vlaanderen | Same word. Activity and any registered marks unknown; Benelux (BOIP) and EUIPO filings need checking. | Seen (company directory listing); activity and marks Unverified |
| 2 | **BELLINE LTD**, United Kingdom, incorporated 11 Aug 2017, wholesale of clothing and footwear | Same word, different trade. Low likelihood of confusion with software, but check for UK marks. | Seen (Companies House) |
| 3 | **BELLINI** (the Cipriani cocktail name) and the Cipriani group's hospitality marks, including Dubai venues | One letter from BELLINE, in the restaurant and hospitality space Belline sells into. The Cipriani family and Belmond have litigated the CIPRIANI name in many countries. Whether BELLINI itself is registered, by whom and in which classes, was not confirmed. | CIPRIANI disputes Seen; BELLINI registrations Unverified |
| 4 | **B-Line AI** / B-Line Technologies (Halifax, Canada), a smart-building platform with an AI assistant | Phonetically close ("bee-line" vs "bell-een" may be read alike in English), and also AI software. Check marks in classes 9 and 42 in the markets Belline targets. | Seen (press release); marks Unverified |
| 5 | **Bell logo similarity**: Bell (Canadian telecom) and other telecom and hospitality brands that use a bell device | Belline's mark is a service bell in a filled circle and sits near telecoms (it answers phone calls). A lawyer should compare device marks in classes 38 and 42 for figurative similarity. | Unverified |
| 6 | Other bell devices in reception, concierge and booking software (for example concierge-bell icons in hotel and restaurant apps) | Crowded field weakens any claim to the bell alone and raises the risk of a device-mark objection. | Unverified |
| 7 | Domains: belline.com reported parked and for sale; belline.ae, belline.io and belline.app reported available | Defensive registration, and whether the .com holder has any marks. | Unverified (from the brand brief; not re-checked here) |

## What was not reachable

- **UAE Ministry of Economy** (trademark e-services): not reachable for search from this environment.
- **EUIPO eSearch plus** and **TMview**: not reachable.
- **WIPO Global Brand Database** (Madrid filings): not reachable.
- USPTO and UK IPO registers were not searched directly either.

## Suggested next steps

1. Lawyer-run clearance search for BELLINE (word) and the bell-button logo (device) in the UAE, GCC, EU, UK and US, classes 9, 35, 38, 42.
2. Specific look at items 1, 3 and 4 above.
3. If clear, file the word mark first, then the combined logo. Do not file the bell pictogram alone.
4. Register belline.ae and the other available domains defensively.
